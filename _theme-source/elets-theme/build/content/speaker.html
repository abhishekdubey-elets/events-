Biography

An officer of the Indian Administrative Service, Akash Tripathi leads two of the institutions that sit closest to India's citizen-facing digital estate: MyGov, the government's citizen engagement platform, and Digital India Corporation, the not-for-profit that builds and runs much of the country's digital public infrastructure.

His remit spans the practical end of Digital India — platform architecture, adoption across states with very different levels of capacity, and the partnerships with industry that make national-scale rollouts possible. That combination is why he tends to appear on the Elets programme where governance meets engineering rather than in purely policy sessions.

On our stages he has consistently pressed the same point: platform capability is the easy part. What determines whether a service reaches the last district is institutional readiness — procurement, staffing and the willingness of a department to change how it works.
