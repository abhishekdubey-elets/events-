Government officers attend free

India's installed capacity has outrun the systems that plan for it. The Expo brings the three parties who have to reconcile that — central policy, state utilities and the technology vendors — into two days of working sessions rather than keynote theatre.

Mornings are plenary: the demand picture, the financing picture, the regulatory calendar. Afternoons split into tracks, each closing with a written takeaway circulated to every delegate within a week. The exhibition floor runs throughout, and the Expo closes with the Energy Excellence Awards.

As with every Elets summit, government officers attend at no cost. The delegate mix is deliberately weighted toward people who sign off on procurement.

Getting there

The Expo is held in central New Delhi, within 40 minutes of Indira Gandhi International Airport and walkable from the nearest Metro station. A shuttle runs between the partner hotels and the venue on both mornings.
